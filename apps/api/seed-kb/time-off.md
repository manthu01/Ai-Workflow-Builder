# Time Off and Leave

Northwind Labs is a fictional company; this handbook is sample data for the
AI Workflow Builder's knowledge-base feature.

## Vacation

Full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Vacation
accrues monthly, at 1.83 days per month, starting on your first day. In your
first year the allowance is prorated from your start date.

You can carry over up to 5 unused vacation days into the next calendar year.
Carried-over days must be used by March 31; any carried-over days still unused
on April 1 are forfeited. Days beyond the 5-day carry-over limit are forfeited
on January 1.

Vacation requests of 3 or more consecutive working days need your manager's
approval at least 2 weeks in advance. Shorter requests need 2 working days'
notice. Requests are made in the HR portal under "Time off".

Part-time employees accrue vacation in proportion to their contracted hours.
Someone contracted for 3 days a week accrues 60% of the full-time allowance.

## Public holidays

The company observes 11 public holidays per year. The exact dates are published
in the HR portal every December for the following year. If a public holiday
falls on a weekend, the next working day is a day off instead.

Employees who are required to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day
off, to be taken within 60 days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is paid and has no fixed annual limit for short illnesses. Tell your
manager as early as possible on the first day you are unwell, before 10:00 if
you can.

An absence of more than 3 consecutive working days requires a doctor's note,
uploaded to the HR portal within 2 working days of your return. Long-term sick
leave of more than 4 weeks is handled case by case with HR and may move to the
company's income-protection insurance after 13 weeks.

You can also use sick leave to care for a sick child or dependant, for up to 5
days per calendar year.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver of a new child, by birth or adoption, receives 18 weeks of
fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 8 weeks of fully
paid leave. Both are available to employees who have completed their probation
period.

Parental leave can start up to 4 weeks before the expected birth or adoption
date, and must be taken within 12 months of it. The secondary caregiver may split
their leave into at most 2 blocks.

After returning from parental leave, employees can work 80% of their hours for
full pay for the first 4 weeks back, as a phased return.

## Other leave

- **Bereavement leave**: 5 paid days for the death of a close family member, and
  2 paid days for other relatives.
- **Moving day**: 1 paid day per year when you move house.
- **Jury duty and civic service**: paid in full for as long as required. Send
  the summons to HR when you receive it.
- **Unpaid leave**: up to 3 months, by agreement with your manager and HR, after
  2 years of employment.
- **Sabbatical**: after 5 years of employment, a one-time 4-week paid sabbatical,
  to be taken within the following 2 years.
