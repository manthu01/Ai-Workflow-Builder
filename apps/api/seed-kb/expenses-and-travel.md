# Expenses and Travel

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase,
with an itemised receipt for every item over 15 euros. Claims submitted between
30 and 60 days after the purchase need your manager's written approval. Claims
older than 60 days are not reimbursed.

Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll if they are
approved by the 20th of the month; otherwise they are paid the month after.

If a receipt is lost, fill in the "missing receipt" declaration in the finance
portal. This is allowed at most 3 times per calendar year.

## What is reimbursed

The company reimburses reasonable costs incurred for work: travel, client
meals, conference fees, and work equipment approved in advance. It does not
reimburse traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment, alcohol outside of
client meals, or upgrades to a higher travel class.

Client meals are reimbursed up to 60 euros per person including tip. Team meals
need a manager's pre-approval and are capped at 40 euros per person.

## Company cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times a year can request a company credit card
from finance. Card purchases still need a receipt uploaded within 30 days. The
card must not be used for personal spending, even if you intend to pay it back.

## Booking travel

All flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal, which
applies the policy limits automatically. Book flights at least 14 days in
advance where possible; bookings made less than 7 days before departure need
your manager's approval.

### Flights

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or
more, premium economy is allowed. Business class is only allowed for flights
over 10 hours and needs director approval.

### Hotels

Hotel stays are capped at 180 euros per night in most cities. The cap is 280
euros per night in London, New York, Tokyo, San Francisco and Zurich. Stays
over the cap need your manager's approval before booking.

### Trains and local transport

Train travel is preferred over flying for journeys under 4 hours. First class
rail is allowed for journeys over 2 hours. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed
when public transport is impractical, for example late at night or with heavy
equipment.

### Per diem

On overnight business trips you receive a per diem of 45 euros per day to cover
meals not otherwise paid for. On the day you travel out and the day you return,
the per diem is 30 euros. If a meal is provided, for example by a conference,
the per diem for that day is reduced by 15 euros per meal.

### Mileage

Business use of a private car is reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre. Your
normal commute is not reimbursed.

## Travel safety

Before travelling to a country marked as high-risk in the travel portal, you
must complete the travel-safety briefing and register your trip with the
security team at least 5 working days before departure.
