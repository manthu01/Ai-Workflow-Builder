# Benefits and Pay

## Payroll

Salaries are paid monthly, on the 25th. If the 25th is a weekend or public
holiday, salary is paid on the previous working day. Payslips are available in
the HR portal from the 23rd.

Salary reviews happen once a year, in April, based on the performance review
cycle that ends in March. Promotions can be proposed at any time and take effect
on the first of the following month.

## Probation

New employees have a 6-month probation period. During probation the notice
period is 2 weeks for both sides. After probation it is 1 month for individual
contributors and 2 months for managers.

## Health insurance

The company pays 100% of the premium for the standard health insurance plan for
employees, and 50% for their partner and children. You can upgrade to the
premium plan and pay the difference through payroll.

Coverage starts on your first day. Changes to your plan, such as adding a
partner, can be made within 30 days of a life event, or otherwise during the
open-enrolment window each November.

## Pension

The company contributes 6% of your base salary to the company pension plan. If
you contribute at least 3% yourself, the company adds a further 2%, for a total
company contribution of 8%. You can join the plan from your first day.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 euros per calendar year for
courses, books, certifications and conferences. Unused budget does not carry
over. Conference travel is paid from the travel budget, not the learning budget.

You can also take up to 5 paid learning days per year for courses or
conferences.

## Wellbeing

Employees get a wellbeing allowance of 40 euros per month for a gym, sports club
or wellness app, paid through payroll. The company also offers free,
confidential counselling: up to 8 sessions per year through the employee
assistance programme.

## Referral bonus

If someone you refer is hired, you receive a referral bonus of 2,000 euros,
paid after they complete their probation period. Referrals for hard-to-fill
roles marked in the careers portal earn 3,000 euros.

## Bike and transit

The company pays 50% of a monthly public transport pass, up to 60 euros per
month. Alternatively you can lease a bike through payroll under the bike-leasing
scheme, with the company paying up to 25 euros per month.
