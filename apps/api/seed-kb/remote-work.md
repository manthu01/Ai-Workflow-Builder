# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Working pattern

Northwind Labs is hybrid by default. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days
per week, and are expected in the office on their team's 2 anchor days. Each
team chooses its anchor days and lists them on the team page in the wiki.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your team's home time zone.
Outside those hours you can arrange your day flexibly, as long as you work your
contracted hours.

## Fully remote roles

Some roles are hired as fully remote. Fully remote employees are invited to an
in-person team week twice a year, with travel and accommodation paid under the
travel policy.

An existing hybrid employee can ask to become fully remote after 12 months in
their role. The request goes to their manager and needs department-head
approval.

## Working from another country

You may work from another country for up to 30 days in any 12-month period,
for example while visiting family. Tell HR at least 2 weeks before you go, so
they can check tax and visa rules. Working from abroad for more than 30 days is
not allowed without a formal employment change.

Working from countries under international sanctions is never allowed.

## Home office setup

Every employee gets a laptop, a monitor, a keyboard and a mouse. On joining you
also receive a one-time home-office stipend of 500 euros for a desk, chair or
other equipment. The stipend is paid with your first month's salary and does not
need receipts.

Equipment bought with the stipend is yours to keep. Equipment the company issues
directly, such as the laptop and monitor, remains company property and must be
returned when you leave.

Fully remote employees also receive an internet allowance of 30 euros per month.

## Coworking spaces

Employees who live more than 50 km from a Northwind office can use a coworking
space and expense up to 250 euros per month for it.

## Availability and communication

When working remotely, keep your chat status up to date and be reachable during
core hours. Use video for meetings of more than two people unless everyone
agrees otherwise. If you will be offline for more than an hour during core
hours, add it to your calendar.
