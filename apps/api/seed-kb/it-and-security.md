# IT and Security

## Accounts and passwords

Your company account uses single sign-on (SSO) for every internal tool.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. You must use the company password
manager for any credential that SSO does not cover. Passwords are not rotated on
a schedule; change yours immediately if you think it has been exposed.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all accounts. Use a hardware
security key or the authenticator app; SMS codes are not accepted.

## Devices

Company laptops have full-disk encryption and the endpoint-protection agent
installed. Do not disable either. Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of
inactivity; lock your screen yourself whenever you step away.

Personal devices may be used for chat and email only, and only through the
official apps with the device-management profile installed. Customer data must
never be stored on a personal device.

Laptops are replaced every 3 years, or sooner if they fail. Request a
replacement through the IT portal.

## Software

You can install software from the self-service catalogue without approval.
Anything not in the catalogue needs an IT request; IT reviews it within 3
working days. Browser extensions that can read page content also need approval.

## Data handling

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer
data is Restricted by default. Restricted data may only be stored in approved
systems, must never be sent by email outside the company, and may only be shared
with people who need it for their work.

Do not paste Confidential or Restricted data into AI tools other than the ones
approved in the IT catalogue.

## Reporting incidents

Report a suspected security incident, such as a phishing email you clicked, a
lost device or a suspected data leak, to the security team immediately, at
security@northwind.example or in the #security-help channel. A lost or stolen
laptop must be reported within 1 hour of noticing. Reporting quickly is never
held against you.

Forward suspicious emails using the "Report phishing" button in your mail
client rather than replying to them.

## Access reviews and offboarding

Managers review their team's access to sensitive systems every quarter. When an
employee leaves, their accounts are disabled at the end of their last working
day and their laptop must be returned within 5 working days.
